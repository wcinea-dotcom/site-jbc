Cayes Botanical Garden

An institution serving biodiversity: data, science, education, and concrete actions in Haiti.

Our Mission

Our mission is to collect and share knowledge and data on biodiversity in order to contribute to human and nature's health.

Two Fundamental Pillars

Our mission is built on two main axes: human health and nature's health.

PILLAR 1
Human Health
Discover the power of medicinal plants, phytotherapy, naturopathy, and nutrition for your health.
Discover
PILLAR 2
Nature's Health
Your health depends on nature: understand ecology, restoration, and conservation. Biodiversity is a priority.
