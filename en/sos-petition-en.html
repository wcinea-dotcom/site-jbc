Save the Cayes Botanical Garden

Sign the petition — 5,000 signatures before June 5, World Environment Day

SIGN THE PETITION

A national scientific heritage is dying in indifference. The Cayes Botanical Garden, built with passion over 23 years, is today forcibly closed. Endemic and native plants are being turned into charcoal. Ecological zones are being eliminated and burned. Some spaces have been turned into banana plantations. Despite the opposition of 8 heirs out of 11, despite appeals to the justice system and State authorities, nothing is moving. Yet the Haitian Constitution of 1987, in its article 256, requires the State to create botanical gardens across the country. This petition is the last call before the total disappearance of this garden, our pride.

Summary of the situation

What is currently happening at the Cayes Botanical Garden.

For over eight months, this garden has been forcibly closed. No child, no student, no researcher, no visitor can enter. Meanwhile, rare plants are disappearing every day. For 23 years, it has been a source of pride for Les Cayes, for Haiti and for the diaspora — a unique place for education, research and discovery of our flora. It is established on six carreaux of land leased from the Gérard family.

In 2018, one heir expressed his intention to sell the entire land. The team then contacted the State to intervene and acquire the property. The State entered into negotiations and requested the necessary titles to include the case in the 2019-2020 national budget. Unfortunately, the documents were never provided, which blocked the process.

In June 2025, while this heir lives in the United States, his wife in Haiti began a process to reclaim the land. This heritage took 23 years to build. She gave only eight full days, with no warning and no prior meeting, to move all the plants.

Eight days later, accompanied by the police and her lawyers, she barred access to the staff. The collections of native and endemic plants were cut down to make charcoal. All species of the ecological garden were systematically eliminated and fire was set over more than one hectare. Banana plantations were installed inside the garden.

Faced with this situation, eight of the eleven heirs, frustrated by this action, wrote to the Minister of Justice and Public Security asking him to intervene urgently. Through their law firm, they also formally ordered Madame to immediately cease all action against the garden.

Our team also reached out to several media outlets. The Minister of the Environment was also contacted. Yet, so far, nothing has been done. Meanwhile, this heritage continues to disappear and the preserved plants are being lost day by day.

This petition aims for 5,000 signatures before June 5, World Environment Day, to demand that the State act urgently: engage in dialogue with the Gérard family to stop the elimination of the country's rare plants, restart the acquisition process, or identify a new site to relocate the garden in the South department — or, failing that, in another department — in accordance with article 256 of the Haitian Constitution.
