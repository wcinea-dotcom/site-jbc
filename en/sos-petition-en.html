Why We Need You Today

Since July 23, 2025, the Cayes Botanical Garden has been closed. For the first time in over 20 years, no child walks through its gates to discover Haitian nature. No student comes to conduct research. No visitor marvels at plants found nowhere else on Earth.

For 23 years, this garden changed lives. Thousands of schoolchildren from Les Cayes touched an endemic leaf for the first time here. Over 15,000 students in agronomy, biology and environmental sciences learned their craft here. Researchers from around the world came to document species they cannot study anywhere else. Over 500,000 visitors felt that rare pride: Haiti holds a living treasure unique in the world.

Today, this place is closed. The team that dedicated their lives to this garden no longer has access to the site. Without care, without water, without daily protection, irreplaceable endemic species are disappearing. Every day that passes adds one year to the time needed to rebuild this living heritage.

The Cayes Botanical Garden — the only botanical garden in Haiti recognized by the Global Network of Botanical Gardens (BGCI) — has been forcibly closed and destroyed since September 3, 2025. Irreplaceable endemic plants are being burned and turned into charcoal. Your signature demands the official reopening on June 5, 2026, World Environment Day.

For every Haitian — whether in Les Cayes, Port-au-Prince, Miami, Montréal or Paris — the Cayes Botanical Garden is a source of pride. Living proof that our country can build, protect, and inspire the world.

Today, this heart is paused. Together, we can make it beat again — stronger than ever.

Our Demands to the Haitian State

1

IMMEDIATE HALT TO ALL DESTRUCTION

Immediate cessation of all destruction activities on the Cayes Botanical Garden site to preserve what remains.

2

SECURITY AND IMMEDIATE ACCESS FOR THE JBC TEAM

Guarantee the safety and immediate access for the JBC team to save the endangered plants suffering every day without care.

3

NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL EXPERT COMMISSION

Establish a national and international expert commission to assess the damage and propose a comprehensive reconstruction plan.

4

OFFICIAL REOPENING ON JUNE 5, 2026

Official decision to reopen the Cayes Botanical Garden on June 5, 2026, World Environment Day.

5

RECONSTRUCTION OF THE JBC AND CREATION OF NEW BOTANICAL GARDENS

Commitment by the State to rebuild the JBC and create new botanical gardens across the territory, in accordance with Article 256 of the Haitian Constitution.
