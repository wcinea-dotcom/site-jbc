The situation — What is happening today

Since July 23, 2025, the Cayes Botanical Garden has been forcibly closed. Mrs. Marie Donna Bastien — wife of one heir of the Gérard family, with no legal mandate over an undivided estate — hired a law firm to evict the JBC team in 8 days. Since then, she has ordered systematic destruction: criminal arson, cutting of plants, production of charcoal, planting of banana trees. The JBC team is barred from entry — even to care for the plants.

Medicinal plants, aromatic plants, rare endemic species — this living heritage belonging to all of Haiti — are being cut down like weeds. The Cereus genus and irreplaceable species disappear every day. Every day that passes adds one year to the time needed to rebuild what has been destroyed. Action is urgent.

These acts are environmental crimes, officially documented by a Justice of the Peace on January 22, 2026. The legitimate heirs of the Gérard family have officially disowned Donna Bastien. The Government Commissioner ordered the garden's reopening on August 13, 2025. That order was ignored.
Without care, without water — the garden suffers and dies. We cannot accept this crime. We cannot remain silent.

"The State has the obligation to create botanical gardens at different points of the national territory for the conservation and enhancement of Haiti's plant resources."
The JBC is the living fulfillment of this constitutional obligation. Destroying it is a crime against the Constitution.

Timeline of events

From the garden's founding to criminal destruction — every act is documented and proven.

2002
🌱 Birth of the JBC

Lease signed with Mrs. Laurette Milard, mother of the Gérard heirs. The garden is born at Bergeaud, Cayes.

Oct. 2018
The Haitian State commits

Official meeting: ANAP + Bruno Gérard (with Donna Bastien present). The State agrees to purchase the land. Bruno Gérard never provides the required documents.

Jul. 23, 2025
Forced closure — without notice

Without any prior letter or meeting, police and a Justice of the Peace physically close the JBC. The team loses all access.

Aug. 13, 2025
🏆 Legal victory — Commissioner's Order

The Government Commissioner ORDERS the resumption of JBC activities. Immediately ignored by Donna Bastien.

Aug. 30, 2025
The order is defied

Donna Bastien changes the barriers, harasses staff, retakes control of the site with her own employees.

Sep. 19, 2025
Heirs officially disown Donna Bastien

The legitimate heirs of the Gérard family officially denounce her. She acts without legal mandate over undivided property.

Dec. 2025 — Mar. 2026
🔥 Massive criminal destruction

Main botanical space set on fire. Endemic plants felled and turned into charcoal. Banana trees planted in their place. Official record by Justice of the Peace Me Paul Tarte — January 22, 2026.

Today
⚠️ Native plants disappear every day

The garden is still closed. The Cereus genus, medicinal and aromatic plants, rare endemic species — all disappearing without care. Every day adds a year to the reconstruction timeline.

Apr. 7, 2026
⚖️ Hearing — Cayes Justice of the Peace Court

JBC lawyers appear with the complete documented case to defend the botanical garden's rights.

Our demands to the Haitian government

1
IMMEDIATE AND TOTAL HALT — ALL ACTIONS BY DONNA BASTIEN AND HER ALLIES

Immediate stop to all activity, destruction, or presence of Mrs. Marie Donna Bastien and her associates at the Cayes Botanical Garden, pending final judgment.

2
IMMEDIATE ACCESS FOR THE JBC TEAM

Allow the JBC team to immediately enter the site to care for the endangered plants suffering every day without care or water.

3
NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL EVALUATION COMMISSION

Creation of a commission of botanical, environmental, and judicial experts — national and international — to assess all damage caused since July 2025 and propose a complete reconstruction plan.

4
OFFICIAL REOPENING ON JUNE 5, 2026

Official decision by the Haitian government for the JBC's reopening on June 5, 2026, World Environment Day — after full damage assessment and start of reconstruction work.

5
IMMEDIATE CRIMINAL PROSECUTION AND EXEMPLARY NATIONAL JUDGMENT

Criminal prosecution under articles 250, 305A, 356, 358, 364 and 365 of the Haitian Penal Code. An exemplary national judgment to ensure these environmental crimes never happen again in Haiti.

6
STATE INVOLVEMENT + RECONSTRUCTION + NEW GARDENS

The Haitian State commits to rebuilding the JBC AND to creating botanical gardens across the entire territory, as required by Article 257 of the Haitian Constitution.
