🌿 The Cayes Botanical Garden Is Under Threat

Since July 23, 2025, the Cayes Botanical Garden has been forcibly closed. For the first time in over 20 years, no child, no student, no researcher, no visitor can pass through its gates to discover Haitian nature.

For 23 years, this garden changed lives. It welcomed thousands of schoolchildren, trained students, inspired researchers, and gave many Haitians — whether in Les Cayes or the diaspora — a reason to be proud. For many, the Cayes Botanical Garden is proof that Haiti can protect its biodiversity, transmit knowledge, and offer the world another image of the country.

Today, this place is under fire by people who do not know the value of it. The team that dedicated their lives to this garden no longer has access to the site. Without care, without water, and without daily protection, rare and endemic plants are disappearing. What is threatened here is not just a garden. It is a living heritage, a scientific memory, a place of education, and a source of hope for an entire generation.

Your signature can make a difference. By signing this petition, you help protect what can still be saved, support the garden's reconstruction, and demand its official reopening on June 5, 2026, World Environment Day.
