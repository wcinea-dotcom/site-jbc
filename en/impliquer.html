GET INVOLVED

Your commitment makes nature grow and protects our biodiversity.

A Shared Science

At the Cayes Botanical Garden (JBC), our vision is to become the reference center for collecting, analyzing, and disseminating high-precision data on nature in Haiti. For this science to truly serve the health of both people and nature, it must be shared, embraced, and valued by society.

Getting involved at the JBC means transforming scientific data into concrete action.

Visit

Discover the various visit options at the Cayes Botanical Garden. Explore beauty, science, and the therapeutic virtues of plants through paths tailored to your needs.

Membership

Supporting the garden means protecting the future. Become a member to enjoy exclusive benefits, access our research, and join our community of experts.

Organize an Event

Transform your professional meetings, celebrations, weddings, or symposiums into exceptional moments in the heart of a unique botanical sanctuary.

Partnership & Experts

We collaborate with institutions and experts to develop programs in ecological restoration, beekeeping, phytochemistry, and naturopathy in Haiti.
