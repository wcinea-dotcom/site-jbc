Cayes Botanical Garden

An institution dedicated to biodiversity: data, science, education and concrete action in Haiti.

Our Mission

Our mission is to collect and share knowledge and data on biodiversity to contribute to the health of people and nature.

Two Fundamental Pillars

Our mission rests on two axes: human health and the health of nature.

Our Areas of Action

We do science and collect data for conservation, horticulture and ecosystem restoration.

Science

Scientific research, botanical studies and ecological monitoring to better understand our biodiversity.

Data

Floristic inventories, maps and databases to guide sound conservation decisions.

Conservation

Concrete actions for the preservation of endemic and native species and natural habitats.

Horticulture & Restoration

Nursery, plant production and solutions to restore the vitality of landscapes.
