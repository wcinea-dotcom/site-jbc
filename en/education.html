DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

We make science accessible to the public: ecology, botany, and plant-based health. We create simple, practical, and inspiring experiences to learn how to better understand nature—and protect it. Our priority: children, to nurture curiosity, creativity, and hope.

OUR MISSION: LEARNING THROUGH NATURE

The Education Department of the Cayes Botanical Garden translates knowledge from research and fieldwork into accessible learning. We build programs that allow people to understand plants, ecosystems, and their direct links to health, food, climate resilience, and conservation.

Our activities give the public concrete benchmarks: observing, identifying, understanding, and taking action. Through workshops and field trips, participants develop practical skills and scientific literacy useful for daily life, environmental careers, and community decision-making.

Our actions are organized around three pillars: school programs · university programs · courses & training.

1. SCHOOL PROGRAMS

Sparking the desire to learn: observation, exploration, practice.

Exploration in the Garden

Guided tours and observation workshops: biodiversity, shapes, scents, uses, and roles of plants.

The Garden at School

Interventions in schools: educational activities, mini-workshops, and awareness-raising on nature protection.

Hands in the Soil

Practical workshops: how to plant, water, protect a young seedling, and understand the role of soil.

2. UNIVERSITY PROGRAMS

Internships, specialized courses, and field-based learning.

Training Internships

Field internships: inventory methods, scientific photography, data collection, and collection work.

Specialized Courses

Applied courses: ecology, botany, ethnobotany, phytotherapy, naturopathy, and other topics related to biodiversity.

Explorations & Inventories

Field trips and floristic inventories to strengthen observation, identification, and analysis skills.
